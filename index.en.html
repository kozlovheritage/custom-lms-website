Why course builders don't always work

GetCourse, Teachable, Kajabi and similar platforms solve one task: launching courses fast. But they come with a cost you keep paying.

%

A cut of every sale

The more successful your project, the more you hand over to the platform — for something you could own yourself.

⚙

Limited logic

Custom tiers, non-standard access scenarios, specific integrations — everything hits "that's not supported."

🔒

Weak content protection

Videos and materials leak, and you can't stop it.

⛓

Dependency

Your customers, payments and content live in someone else's ecosystem. Terms change — you adapt.

If your project has outgrown the builder, it's time to own the platform instead of renting it.

A platform that covers the entire customer journey

From the first sale to repeat purchases — all in one system that belongs to you.

Sales site + payments

Pricing pages, payment processing, automatic access delivery right after checkout. One or two payment gateways — so sales don't stop if something goes wrong.

Member portal

Customers see their plan, access expiry and the content catalog: video, PDF, text. Works well on desktop and mobile.

Content protection

Video is protected from downloading and tied to the user's session. Materials can't simply be "taken" and resold.

Access automation

Payment confirmed — access granted. No manual work, no risk of a customer paying and getting nothing.

Admin panel

Payments, user statuses, granting and revoking access, key metrics — all in one place.

Post-launch support

The platform isn't "delivered and forgotten": I monitor stability, fix issues, and develop it as the project grows.

How it works in production

"Fabrika Zagotovok" is a culinary club for which I built the combination of a sales site and a closed learning platform. This isn't a prototype — it runs with real people and real payments.

What was built

  • Two tiers: the basic one leads to a private Telegram club, the premium one to the web platform.
  • Payments through a primary and a backup gateway — if one is down, sales continue.
  • Automatic access delivery after payment, with duplicate-payment protection.
  • A member portal with a content catalog: video, PDF collections, text.
  • Session-bound protected video.
  • An admin panel: payments, access, metrics.
